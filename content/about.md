I'm a Computer Engineering student and a Cybersecurity Analyst based in Buenos Aires, specialized in Security Operations and threat intelligence. I created this blog in order to keep a record of my projects, what I've learned along the way, and share my thought process with others.

## Background

Earlier this year, I started exploring [Cisco](https://www.netacad.com/)'s free courses and took Introduction to Cybersecurity as a personal challenge. By the end of it, the field felt a lot more approachable than before, and it drew my interest toward Blue Teaming and defensive security.

I went on to complete their Junior Cybersecurity Analyst Career Path, alongside a plethora of other courses. More recently, I have completed a SOC L2 bootcamp from [ComunidadDOJO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/comunidaddojo/), which is where much of the hands-on work on this blog comes from.
